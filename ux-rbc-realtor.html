REALTOR.ca

RBC × REALTOR.ca Exit Card

Redesigning a partner-branded exit card when the brand guidelines and the funnel disagreed.

Role Interaction Designer
Duration 2 weeks
Team HomeX
Methods UX Design
Prototype
Stakeholder Alignment

Design Phase 1: Empathize

Background

A partnership card that satisfies brand guidelines

During my internship on RBC's Mortgage Pre-Approval Tool team, we ran a referral partnership with REALTOR.ca. Users interested in getting pre-approved could click through from REALTOR.ca straight into our tool, and our agreement required an exit path back.

Referral round trip
REALTOR.ca Pre-approval Confirmation Result Exit card

Where this started

Updating the Houseful cards in our confirmation and result pages

The starting point looked simple. My task was to redesign that card to follow REALTOR.ca's branding guidelines instead.

Design Phase 2: Define

Brand guidelines vs. readability

REALTOR.ca's wider margins conflicted with our existing card component

Applied strictly, the logo would render too small to read clearly inside our existing card component. That read as unprofessional, and worse, as unusable.

I raised it with my mentor and proposed an adjusted layout, then submitted it to REALTOR.ca as a request for an exception to their standard margin rule.

Brand colour and drop-off

REALTOR.ca's bold red might worsen the drop-off

Showing that card on the confirmation page risked compounding a problem the team was already tracking. Many users stopped at the confirmation page and never reached the result page, mistakenly assuming the flow had ended.

A highly visible red card at that point could add to the drop-off rather than help it.

Design Phase 3: Ideate

Proposing solutions

I brought two options to the team

Option 1 was to keep the card on the confirmation page but recolour it to REALTOR.ca's secondary black, so it stopped competing with RBC's blue. Lower development cost, but it would not meaningfully address the drop-off.

Option 2 was to remove the exit card from the confirmation page entirely and show it only on the result page. That required new logic to detect REALTOR.ca referrals, and a resize of the remaining card to full width on the confirmation page for visual balance.

Design Phase 4: Prototype & Deliver

Outcome

REALTOR.ca chose option 2 and accepted the margin exception

I then worked with our developer and visual designer to select a full-width card design and implement the logic end to end.
