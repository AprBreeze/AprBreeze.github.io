LEVEL DESIGNER INTERN关卡设计师实习

Perfect World 7th Project Team完美世界 第七项目组

Project Overview项目概览

During my internship at Perfect World Studio, I contributed to the design of open-world exploration systems, blockout standards, and specialized gameplay challenges for a open-world project. My responsibilities involved translating high-level design goals into functional gameplays and level design prototypes.在完美世界实习期间，我参与了一个开放世界项目的探索系统设计、白盒标准和专项玩法挑战。我的工作是把高层设计目标落成可玩的玩法和关卡原型。


Due to NDA, I can't directly share the content. The following section will outline my key tasks and achievements during this internship.因保密协议，具体内容无法直接展示。下面概括这次实习的主要任务和成果。

Key Tasks and Achievements主要任务与成果

Open-World Design开放世界设计

  • Crafted exploration loops that integrate combat, puzzles, and resource rewards, ensuring meaningful and rewarding player experiences.设计把战斗、解谜和资源奖励串起来的探索循环，让玩家的投入有明确回报。
  • Learned the 360-degree approach to open-world design, ensuring players can enter areas from any direction while maintaining a consistent and engaging experience.学习开放世界的 360度探索设计方式，保证玩家从任意方向进入区域时，体验仍然完整、一致。
  • Focused on creating content-rich environments to maintain open-world exploration engagement.注重把环境做充实，维持开放世界探索的投入感。

Blockout Prototyping白盒原型

  • Helped develop and document blockout construction standards to streamline workflows and align team efforts.参与制定并记录白盒搭建标准，统一流程，让团队协作更顺。
  • Emphasized modularity and scalability during prototyping, enabling rapid iteration and consistent results across levels.原型阶段强调模块化和可扩展，方便快速迭代，并在不同关卡间保持一致结果。

Gameplay Challenge Development玩法挑战开发

  • Designed engaging gameplay challenges, incorporating player mobility, verticality, and spatial exploration.设计有吸引力的玩法挑战，结合玩家移动、垂直空间和空间探索。
  • Collaborated with programmers to ensure challenges were functional both offline and online.与程序协作，保证挑战在离线和在线环境下都能正常运行。

Design Approach and Iterative Process设计方法与迭代流程

  • Followed a player-first approach, focusing on how mechanics and layouts enhance player agency and immersion.以玩家为先：关注机制和布局如何增强玩家主动性和沉浸感。
  • Iteratively refined designs by integrating feedback from lead designers and cross-functional teams between programmers, artists, and QAs.结合主策、程序、美术和 QA 的反馈，反复打磨设计。
  • Used a test-and-learn approach to evaluate and adjust gameplay elements for balance and technical feasibility.用试玩验证、再调整的方式，兼顾平衡与技术可行性。

Spatial Design and Player Flow空间设计与玩家动线

  • Designed layouts with verticality variance to promote strategic exploration and player creativity.用垂直高度差做布局，推动策略性探索和玩家发挥。
  • Prioritized player flow by optimizing navigation, pacing, and accessibility in large-scale environments using the concept of positive and negative space.在大尺度环境中用正负空间优化导航、节奏和可达性，优先保证玩家动线。

Technical and Analytical Skills技术与分析能力

  • Debugged and tested gameplay mechanics to ensure stability, particularly in online environments.调试并测试玩法机制，尤其关注在线环境下的稳定性。
  • Aligned designs with technical constraints while maintaining creative intent and gameplay goals.在技术限制内对齐设计，同时保住创意意图和玩法目标。
