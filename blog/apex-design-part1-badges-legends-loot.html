How System Design in APEX Kept High Player Engagement《Apex英雄》的系统设计如何维持高玩家参与

This piece is adapted from a video script I originally wrote back in 2021. Some systems and specific numbers may have changed since then, but it still reflects the design thinking behind the original analysis.本文改编自我 2021 年写的视频脚本。之后部分系统和具体数值可能已变，但仍反映当时分析背后的设计思路。

According to a report from Dexerto, Apex Legends had a landmark fiscal year in 2022. For a game that launched back in 2019, it still commands a large and active core player base, and FY2022 was in fact the year the game brought in the most new players since launch.据 Dexerto 报道，《Apex英雄》在 2022 财年表现突出。作为 2019 年上线的游戏，它仍拥有庞大且活跃的核心玩家群，而 FY2022 实际上是上线以来吸引新玩家最多的一年。

Seasonal events, heirloom drops, and battle passes all help keep players engaged, but none of that matters if the underlying gameplay isn't good. A game survives long term because of a play loop that's genuinely replayable and worth investing time in. For a first person battle royale shooter, I'd argue that fun comes down to three core pleasures: the randomness of looting, the tension of engagements, and the strategy of survival. Apex balances these three elements carefully across a huge number of small design decisions, and that balance is what lets its core loop hold up over time. This piece is the first in a short series looking at how.赛季活动、传家宝掉落和战斗通行证都能维持参与，但底层玩法不好，这些都没用。游戏能长线存活，靠的是真正可重玩、值得投入时间的核心循环。对第一人称大逃杀射击来说，我认为乐趣落在三种核心快感：搜刮的随机、交火的紧张、生存的策略。《Apex英雄》在大量细小设计决策里仔细平衡这三者，正是这种平衡让核心循环经得起时间。本文是短系列的第一篇，拆解它是怎么做到的。

Badges and Scoreboards: Turning Skill Into a Visible Target徽章与记分板：把技术变成看得见的目标

The most surface level, most obvious system in Apex is one players see before the match even starts, back in the drop ship: the badge and scoreboard system.《Apex英雄》里最表层、也最显眼的系统，玩家在开局前、还在空投飞艇上就能看到：徽章与记分板系统。

Before a single shot is fired, Apex shows players a rundown of their squad's stats per legend and a badge case. Lifetime kills is one of the clearest signals of a player's experience and skill level, and the badges speak for themselves. Countless players die and respawn over and over chasing the next tier of badge. Damage badges (the 2K badge, the 2.5K badge, and so on up to the 4K badge, informally known as the "double hatchet") and the 20 Bomb kill badge have become long running goals for competitive players. These are some of the hardest badges in the game to earn: a full suit of the best armor in the game only gives a player 225 combined shield and health, so reaching 4,000 cumulative damage in one match, or getting more than 20 kills out of 60 total players in a lobby, is genuinely difficult.一枪未开，《Apex英雄》就展示小队每位传奇的数据概览和徽章陈列。生涯击杀是玩家经验与技术水平最清晰的信号之一，徽章本身也很说明问题。无数玩家一次次阵亡、重生，只为追下一档徽章。伤害徽章（2K、2.5K，一路到俗称「双锤」的 4K）以及 20 Bomb 击杀徽章，早成了竞技向玩家的长期目标。这些是游戏里最难拿的徽章之一：一身顶级护甲合计也只有 225 护盾加生命，所以单局累积 4000 伤害，或从 60 人局里打出超过 20 个击杀，确实很难。

That difficulty is the point. Apex sets these as official benchmarks so players who enjoy the grind always have something to chase, and a way to show it off once they get there. A new player works toward the 2K badge, and once they clear it, the 2.5K badge, only 500 damage away, becomes the obvious next target. Every skill level has its own next milestone, and rarer badges give players more reason to keep logging hours, not to mention something to flex on other players with.难，正是设计目的。《Apex英雄》把它们设成官方标尺，让爱肝的玩家永远有东西追，拿到后也有东西炫耀。新手冲 2K，清掉之后只差 500 伤害的 2.5K 就成了下一个明显目标。每个技术段位都有下一座里程碑，更稀有的徽章让人更愿意持续上线，当然也能拿来秀。

The flip side is that because these badges are genuinely hard to earn in fair matches, they've also incentivized cheating, boosting, and lobby stomping (deliberately queuing into much weaker lobbies). That's a real problem, but it's common across competitive shooters and hard to isolate as a badge specific effect, so it's outside the scope of this piece.反面是：这些徽章在公平对局里确实难拿，也就刺激了作弊、代练上分，以及炸鱼（故意匹配进弱很多的对局碾压）。这是真问题，但在竞技射击里很常见，也很难单独归因于徽章系统，所以本文不展开。

Giving players goals and challenges is close to a universal principle in game design, and Apex's badge system leans hard on competitiveness and the desire to clear the next hurdle. It converts the simple act of playing more into a steady stream of achievable milestones.给玩家目标和挑战，几乎是游戏设计的通用原则；《Apex英雄》的徽章系统狠狠押在竞争欲、以及想跨过下一关的冲动上。它把「多玩」这件事，转成一连串可达成的里程碑。

Legend Diversity: Making Team Composition a Real Decision传奇多样性：让阵容选择成为真决策

Character differentiation is the next layer. Every legend in Apex differs in silhouette, hitbox size, and tactical kit, and squads can't run duplicate legends. That single restriction forces real composition decisions: a squad might build around aggressive, fight seeking legends to force engagements, or lean into a defensive "turtle" comp anchored by Wattson, Caustic, and Rampart to hold down a building.下一层是角色差异。每位传奇在剪影、判定体积和战术技能组上都不同，且小队不能重复选同一传奇。这一条限制就逼出真阵容决策：可以围绕强势找架的传奇组队逼战，也可以用 Wattson、Caustic、Rampart 撑起偏防守的「乌龟」阵容死守建筑。

Because tactical abilities vary so much from legend to legend, they add real tactical and strategic variety to a genre that can otherwise blur together fast. Every fight forces a squad to react differently depending on both the enemy composition and their own, which adds a layer of strategic depth that a lot of FPS battle royales lack.传奇之间战术技能差异很大，给本容易同质化的品类补上真实的战术与战略变化。每场战斗都要按敌我阵容做出不同应对，多出一层许多 FPS 大逃杀缺少的策略深度。

Fewer Loot Items, More Built-In Abilities更少战利品道具，更多内建技能

One thing that stands out about Apex compared to other shooters is how few tactical items are actually available to loot. Most of that functionality has instead been folded into the legend ability system in a more varied form. That's also, I think, why Apex rarely gives legends abilities with high direct lethality: the point of the ability system isn't to raise any one legend's damage ceiling, it's to add battlefield read and real time decision making to combat.和别的射击游戏比，《Apex英雄》一个突出点是：能搜到的战术道具其实很少。这类功能大多以更丰富的形式收进了传奇技能系统。我想这也是为什么《Apex英雄》很少给传奇高直接杀伤的技能：技能系统的重点不是抬高某一传奇的伤害上限，而是给战斗加上战场阅读和实时决策。

Everything else about Apex's design points toward the same goal: encourage team play and reduce how much individual skill alone can swing a fight. That makes the game easier to balance and narrows the gap between skill tiers, which lowers the barrier to entry (more on that another time).《Apex英雄》其余设计也指向同一目标：鼓励团队配合，削弱单靠个人技术就能左右一战的程度。这让游戏更好平衡，也缩小技术段位差距，从而降低入门门槛（这点另文再写）。

Back to abilities specifically: because tactical items were folded into legend kits, every player drops into the map already equipped with their own tactical tool. That cuts down on early game loot time, speeds up the pace of a match, and puts more weight on positioning, target selection, and overall play rather than what you happen to find on the ground. It's Respawn subtracting from the blunt randomness of early battle royale design: keep the fun of randomness, but compress it into a controllable range, which raises competitive fairness. In plain terms, an average player can no longer blame a bad landing spot as often, and a great player is less often blindsided by a random gear disadvantage.回到技能本身：战术道具被收进传奇技能组后，每位玩家落地时就自带战术工具。这缩短了开局搜刮时间，加快对局节奏，也更看重站位、目标选择和整体操作，而不是碰巧地上捡到什么。这是 Respawn 在削弱早期大逃杀那种粗暴随机：保留随机的乐趣，但压进可控区间，从而提高竞技公平。说白了，普通玩家更难总拿落点背锅，高手也更少被随机装备劣势突然阴到。

That same design philosophy, preserving the thrill of randomness while keeping it within controllable bounds, runs all the way through one of Apex's most important systems: the EVO Shield. That's where the next piece in this series picks up.同一套设计哲学——保留随机的刺激，又把它关在可控边界里——贯穿《Apex英雄》最重要的系统之一：进化甲。系列下一篇就从这里接上。
