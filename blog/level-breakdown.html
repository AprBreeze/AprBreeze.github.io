Learn from Systematically Breaking Down Levels从系统拆解关卡中学习

Today, after a lot of research and organizing information online, I realized there isn’t a convincing structure for systematically breaking down game levels. Some say breakdowns should involve listing all elements and identifying their design purposes. However, I feel this approach lacks guidance for level design because level design is a complex craft where different dimensions vary greatly yet are interconnected.今天查了很多资料、整理网络上的说法之后，我发现并没有一套足够令人信服、能系统拆解游戏关卡的结构。有人说拆解就是列出所有元素并指出各自设计目的。但我觉得这对关卡设计帮助不够：关卡设计很复杂，不同维度差异很大，又彼此咬合。

Some say breakdowns should involve listing all elements and identifying their design purposes. However, I feel this approach lacks guidance for level design because level design is a complex craft where different dimensions vary greatly yet are interconnected. If I break down everything to the smallest units like a system designer, I lose the high-level/cohesive perspective that a level designer needs.有人说拆解就是列出所有元素并指出各自设计目的。但我觉得这对关卡设计帮助不够：关卡设计很复杂，不同维度差异很大，又彼此咬合。 如果像系统设计师那样拆到最小单元，我会丢掉关卡设计师需要的那种整体视角。

That said, I've been facing repeated setbacks in interviews, and I have to admit that my ability to deconstruct existing products is objectively lacking, something interviewers care about a lot.话说回来，我面试里反复碰壁，也得承认自己拆解现有产品的能力客观上还不够，而这正是面试官很看重的。

A New Framework for Level Analysis一套新的关卡分析框架

So today, I tried combining online insights with my own level design workflow and reflections, and I created a structure for level breakdowns that feels right to me.所以今天，我把网上的观点和自己的关卡设计流程、反思合在一起，整理出一套我觉得合适的关卡拆解结构。

I divided level design into four sections. Although these areas overlap, separating them helps keep the analysis clear:我把关卡设计分成四块。虽然彼此重叠，但拆开能让分析更清楚：

1. Mechanics1. 机制

  • Gameplay type - Core gameplay mechanics and their variations玩法类型 - 核心玩法机制及其变体
  • Experience goals - What the level aims to achieve体验目标 - 关卡想达成什么
  • Design constraints - Technical and gameplay limitations设计约束 - 技术与玩法上的限制

2. Narrative2. 叙事

  • Narrative pacing - How the story unfolds through the level叙事节奏 - 故事如何在关卡中展开
  • Spatial storytelling - How the macro-level scene content tells the story空间叙事 - 宏观场景内容如何讲故事

3. Space3. 空间

  • Path guidance - How players are directed through the level路径引导 - 玩家如何被引导穿过关卡
  • Spatial storytelling - How micro-level content layout supports the narrative空间叙事 - 微观内容布局如何支撑叙事

4. Pacing4. 节奏

  • Experience flow - The rhythm and flow of gameplay moments体验动线 - 玩法时刻的韵律与流动
  • Experience intensity - The peaks and valleys of player engagement体验强度 - 玩家投入的波峰与波谷

This analysis structure matches my actual level design workflow, but the order is slightly different.这套分析结构对应我实际的关卡设计流程，但顺序略有不同。

When designing levels, I usually prioritize pacing first — defining the level's rhythm (including narrative pacing) — before working on space (2D layouts and 3D blockouts).做关卡时，我通常先定节奏——定义关卡韵律（含叙事节奏）——再去做空间（2D 布局和 3D 白盒）。

However, for breakdowns, I adjusted the order: since narrative and space are more objective and visible, analyzing them first makes the subsequent pacing analysis more accurate and grounded.但做拆解时，我调了顺序：叙事和空间更客观、更看得见，先分析它们，后面的节奏分析才更准确、更有依据。

Moving Forward接下来

Moving forward, I plan to use this framework to break down levels and seek feedback from industry veterans to validate my approach. This structured method should help me better understand existing games and improve my ability to discuss level design in professional contexts.接下来，我打算用这套框架拆解关卡，并向行业前辈征求反馈来验证方法。结构化的做法，应能帮我更好理解现有游戏，也提升在职业语境里讨论关卡设计的能力。
