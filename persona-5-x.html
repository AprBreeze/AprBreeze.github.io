Design Intent设计意图

Three goals drove every layout and beat decision:三个目标驱动了每一次布局与节拍决策：

  • Scale & tension — Make the Boss feel massive and the shrunken party genuinely vulnerable体量与张力 - 让 Boss 显得巨大，让缩小后的队伍真正感到脆弱
  • Thematic clarity — Use the gift pile to express vanity and overindulgence (the enemy spoiled by her parents)主题清晰 - 用礼物堆表达虚荣与过度纵容（被父母溺爱的敌人）
  • Experience vs. cost — Balance visual impact, clean camera coverage, narrative service, and interactive traversal without scope blowout体验与成本 - 在视觉冲击、干净镜头覆盖、叙事服务与可互动动线之间取得平衡，避免范围失控

Greybox Iteration白盒迭代

The first greybox already tried to control cost, but the flow stayed structurally complex. Art polish and camera validation would have consumed disproportionate time.第一版白盒已经在控制成本，但动线结构仍然偏复杂。美术精修与镜头验证会吃掉不成比例的时间。

After syncing with art on tight resourcing, I led a second pass: simplified layout complexity, reused existing assets wherever possible, and kept the core beats: boss proximity, stealth tension, thematic read, and traversal interaction.与美术对齐紧张资源后，我主导了第二轮：简化布局复杂度，尽量复用已有资产，并保留核心节拍：Boss 近距离压迫、潜行张力、主题可读性，以及可互动的动线。

  • V1 — Full-flow proof of concept; validated pacing but flagged high art costV1 - 完整动线概念验证；节奏成立，但美术成本偏高
  • V2 — Production-ready simplification; same player experience, lower build riskV2 - 可投产的简化版；玩家体验不变，构建风险更低

Narrative, Dialogue & Cinematics叙事、台词与演出

Part 2 — Design Thinking第 2 部分 · 设计思考

2.1 Starting From What the Player Needs2.1 从玩家需要什么出发

Before I touch a single mechanic, I try to name what the player actually needs in that moment. In P5X that meant light cognitive load paired with narrative texture, since side quests exist to give players a breather between main story beats, not to test their patience.动手做机制前，我先尽量说清玩家此刻真正需要什么。在《女神异闻录：夜幕魅影》里，这意味着轻认知负荷加上叙事质感：支线是给主线节拍之间喘口气，不是考验耐心。

I research comparable puzzle references, then translate that target into a concrete design goal before sketching anything. That target became my filter for later decisions, including which mechanics to cut when they got too expensive to feel worth it.我会先研究可比的解谜参考，再把目标落成具体设计目标，然后才开始草图。这个目标成了后续决策的过滤器，包括当机制成本过高、不值得时该砍掉什么。

2.2 Core Puzzle Principle2.2 核心解谜原则

Before designing any specific puzzle, I studied how comparable puzzle games, including several other gacha titles, build moments of intentional misdirection. That research shaped two principles.在设计具体谜题前，我研究了可比解谜游戏（包括几款其他抽卡产品）如何做有意误导。这些研究沉淀成两条原则。

The first is feedback. A player should always be able to tell, at a glance, whether an action is moving them closer to the answer. That only works if the puzzle's scope is unambiguous, with no stray elements nearby that could be mistaken for part of the solution.第一条是反馈。玩家一眼就该能判断，某个操作是不是在靠近答案。这只有在谜题范围明确、附近没有会被误认成解法一部分的杂项时才成立。

The second is contradiction. A good puzzle deliberately leads the player toward a first instinct, then makes sure that instinct is wrong. That gap between what feels right and what is actually right becomes the puzzle's core conflict.第二条是矛盾。好谜题会故意把玩家引向第一直觉，再确认那直觉是错的。感觉对与实际对之间的落差，就是谜题的核心冲突。

The wrong turn itself has to feel safe, not punishing, so it reads as an invitation to rethink rather than a failure to recover from.错误路径本身要感觉安全、不惩罚，读起来像邀请玩家重新思考，而不是需要从失败里爬回来。

2.3 Building Puzzles Out of Modules2.3 用模块拼谜题

I build small, self-contained puzzle modules first, then assemble them into the full quest. Each module solves one idea on its own, which makes it easy to test in isolation, swap out if it doesn't land, or reuse elsewhere.我先做小而自洽的解谜模块，再组装成完整任务。每个模块单独解决一个想法，方便隔离测试、不成立时替换，或复用到别处。

This habit paid off most on the longer quests, where a single room needing rework didn't mean reworking the whole sequence.这个习惯在长任务上最管用：单个房间要返工，不等于整段流程都要重做。

Modular assembly模块化组装

2.4 Script Before Space2.4 先写剧本，再做空间

I write dialogue and narrative beats before laying out the physical space. Text is the cheapest thing to revise, and writing it first forces me to know what feeling a room is supporting before I commit to its geometry.我会先写台词与叙事节拍，再铺物理空间。文本是最便宜的修改对象；先写它，能逼我在敲定几何前先弄清房间要支撑的感觉。

Every quest followed the same order: script, then space, then mechanics, then iteration, each stage getting more expensive to change than the last.每条任务都按同一顺序：剧本 → 空间 → 机制 → 迭代，每一阶段的改动成本都比上一阶段更高。

Production order & rising cost of change制作顺序与上升的改动成本

2.5 Designing Behavior Tree Logic2.5 设计行为树逻辑

Mechanisms in P5X talk to each other through behavior trees, and a chunk of my time went into untangling that logic: selectors, sequences, parallel branches, and how a change in one node ripples into the next.《女神异闻录：夜幕魅影》里的机制通过行为树互相通信，我花了不少时间理清这套逻辑：选择器、序列、并行分支，以及一个节点的改动如何波及下一个。

A representative example: a puzzle where lighting the correct lantern color sends a guard away, which only then increments a chest's interaction count from zero to one, unlocking it. Cases like that taught me to think in states and transitions, not just trigger and effect.一个代表性例子：点亮正确颜色的灯笼会支开守卫，这才会把宝箱交互计数从 0 加到 1，从而解锁。这类案例让我学会用状态与转换思考，而不只是触发与效果。

Behavior tree logic (square = condition, diamond = selector)行为树逻辑（方块 = 条件，菱形 = 选择器）

2.6 Letting Testing Find What the Document Couldn't2.6 让测试找出文档看不出的问题

Every quest taught me something during playtesting that the design document hadn't surfaced. A floating object needed a physical platform once art got involved.每条任务在试玩里都教给我设计文档没暴露的东西。美术介入后，一个漂浮物体需要实体平台支撑。

A puzzle state read as a bug instead of a clue until I rewrote the failure feedback to be specific instead of vague. I treat early playtests as the real first draft of a puzzle, not a final check.有个解谜状态一度被读成 bug 而不是线索，直到我把失败反馈从模糊改成具体。我把早期试玩当成谜题真正的初稿，而不是终检。

Playtesting feedback loop试玩反馈闭环

Part 3 — What Stuck With Me第 3 部分 · 留下的收获

  • Remind, not recall. Tell the player what a tool does the moment they need it, instead of expecting them to remember a rule from ten minutes earlier.提醒，而不是回忆。 在玩家需要时立刻告诉他们工具做什么，而不是指望他们记住十分钟前的规则。
  • Separate what can't change from what can. Cross-department work goes smoothly when I'm clear about my non-negotiables and leave everything else open to other teams' expertise.分清什么不能改、什么可以改。 跨部门协作顺利的前提，是我清楚自己的不可妥协点，并把其余空间留给其他组的专业判断。
  • Design isn't validated until someone else plays it. The clearest problems and the best ideas both showed up during testing, not at my desk.设计要等别人玩过才算验证。 最清楚的问题和最好的想法，都出现在测试里，而不是我的工位上。
  • One task at a time. A sticky note system that lives outside my screen does more for my focus than trying to multitask inside it.一次只做一件事。 一套活在屏幕外的便利贴系统，比在屏幕里硬多任务更能帮我集中注意力。
